# Problem Discovery Report
## SkillCraft Micro-Jobs: A Hyper-Local Platform for Rural Micro-Artisans

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Rural micro-artisans and blue-collar workers—handloom weavers, plumbers, electricians, carpenters, tailors, and masons—operate in a digital void. They possess valuable skills and can earn decent livelihoods, yet they have **no formal, transparent platform** to connect directly with customers and employers.

The informal economy in India accounts for over 93% of employment (ILO 2023). Within this sector:

- **Handloom weavers** in places like Bhagalpur, Varanasi, and Salem struggle to sell directly; middlemen take 30–50% commission.
- **Plumbers, electricians, and carpenters** in small towns rely entirely on word-of-mouth recommendations and WhatsApp groups, with no accountability or formal work history.
- **Tailors** cannot differentiate themselves by past work quality without a credible rating system.
- **Laborers and artisans** have no escrow-style payment protection when working with strangers.

### The Gap

**Existing platforms fail rural artisans:**
- **LinkedIn & Naukri**: Designed for white-collar, office-based roles; rural micro-artisans cannot meaningfully list handloom weaving or carpentry.
- **Urban Company & Amazon Home Services**: Require formal business registration and corporate affiliation; exclude informal workers.
- **Fiverr & Upwork**: Geared toward digital services; do not support location-based, physical-world gigs.
- **WhatsApp & Informal Networks**: Zero accountability; no work history, no dispute resolution, no payment proof.

---

## 2. Affected Groups & Stakeholders

### Primary Users

1. **Artisans (Supply Side)**
   - Rural handloom weavers, electricians, plumbers, carpenters, tailors, masons
   - Typically earn ₹100–300/hour informally; lose 30–50% to middlemen
   - Target: Retain **50–70% more earnings** by eliminating middlemen
   - Located in small towns and villages across India

2. **Employers (Demand Side)**
   - Urban individuals and small businesses seeking skilled labor
   - Currently overpay due to middleman markups or accept low-quality informal work
   - Target: Access **vetted, rated artisans with transparent pricing**

3. **Platform (Operator)**
   - Acts as a trust intermediary and transaction facilitator
   - Handles payment escrow, dispute resolution, and quality assurance

### Secondary Stakeholders

- **Local Governments**: Benefit from formalized tax collection and worker registration
- **Artisan Cooperatives**: Can use the platform to group artisans and negotiate bulk contracts
- **Microfinance Institutions**: Can use platform transaction history to assess creditworthiness

## 3. Evidence & Problem Validation

### Primary Research

- **Interviews with 12 handloom weavers** (Bhagalpur, March 2024): 100% reported losing 30–50% of earnings to middlemen; 75% expressed willingness to use a platform if trusted.
- **Survey of 25 urban households** (Delhi, Pune, Bengaluru, April 2024): 92% have struggled to find reliable skilled labor; 88% would use a platform offering verified artisans.

### Secondary Research

- ILO "Women and Men in the Informal Economy" (2023): 93% of Indian employment is informal; women make up 66% of informal workers.
- Government of India "National Policy on Skill Development & Entrepreneurship" (2015): Emphasizes need for formal skill tracking and certification in rural areas.
- Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI): 421 million broadband subscribers in India (Jan 2024); rural penetration reached 38%.

### Economic Impact

- **Middleman Commission Loss**: A weaver earning ₹2,500/week loses ₹750–1,250 to middlemen annually ≈ ₹39,000–65,000/year
- **Scale Opportunity**: ~10 million micro-artisans in India; even 5% adoption = 500,000 users
- **Potential Earnings Uplift**: If platform captures 2.5% (transaction fee) and artisans retain 75% of proceeds, artisans gain **~₹39,000/year** vs. current informal earnings

## 4. Existing Solutions Studied

### A. LinkedIn

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **For Artisans** | Not applicable; profile must emphasize office credentials |
| **Skill Discovery** | Keywords only; no location-based or category-based matching |
| **Payment** | None; external negotiation |
| **Rating System** | Endorsements; not transaction-based |
| **Why Inadequate** | Designed for salaried, white-collar roles; no gig/project matching |

### B. Naukri.com

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **For Artisans** | Requires formal CV; micro-trades not searchable |
| **Skill Discovery** | Job postings for permanent/contract roles; no one-off gigs |
| **Payment** | Salary negotiation; no escrow |
| **Rating System** | None; employer interviews only |
| **Why Inadequate** | Focuses on employment, not freelance gigs; no artisan-friendly categories |

### C. Urban Company

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **For Artisans** | Requires business registration + 5-star rating before first job |
| **Skill Discovery** | Categories available but limited; high barrier to entry |
| **Payment** | UC handles escrow but takes 20–30% commission |
| **Rating System** | Customer reviews; penalizes artisans heavily for single bad review |
| **Why Inadequate** | Middleman model persists; high commission for artisans; formal registration discourages informal workers |

### D. Fiverr & Upwork

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **For Artisans** | No support for physical/location-based services |
| **Skill Discovery** | Digital services only (writing, design, programming) |
| **Payment** | Escrow available but with 20%+ platform fee |
| **Rating System** | Detailed reviews but anonymized |
| **Why Inadequate** | Cannot sell handloom weaving, plumbing, or carpentry |

### E. WhatsApp + Informal Networks

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **For Artisans** | Zero friction to entry; minimal exposure beyond network |
| **Skill Discovery** | Word-of-mouth only; zero search capability |
| **Payment** | Cash on completion; no recourse if payment fails |
| **Rating System** | Reputation based on rumor; no permanent record |
| **Why Inadequate** | No accountability, no protection, no geographic reach, impossible to scale trust |

## 5. Identified Gap & Innovation

### The Gap

**No digital platform exists that:**
1. **Accepts informal artisans without corporate affiliation**
2. **Matches artisans to employers by skill + location** with real-time search
3. **Holds payment in escrow** until work completion
4. **Computes trust scores from verified transaction history** (not self-reported reviews)
5. **Provides transparent, permanent work history** that artisans can leverage for future gigs
6. **Supports regional languages** for non-English-speaking artisans
7. **Charges minimal commission** (< 10%) vs. traditional middlemen (30–50%)

### SkillCraft Micro-Jobs Innovation

**SkillCraft removes the middleman and democratizes access to skill-matching:**

| Innovation | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Direct Employer Connection** | Artisans keep 90–95% of negotiated price (vs. 50–70% with middleman) |
| **Skill + Location Matching** | Artisans found by employers searching for specific trades in their town |
| **Transparent Bidding** | Artisans bid; employers choose; no hidden markups |
| **Trust Score from Verified Contracts** | Score based on payment completion + review stars, not self-reported |
| **Payment Escrow** | Platform holds payment; releases only after both parties confirm |
| **Regional Language Support** | Artisans in Bhagalpur (Hindi), Salem (Tamil), Surat (Gujarati) all welcomed |
| **Minimal Commission** | Platform takes 5–7% to sustain operations; artisans gain 85–90% retention vs. current 50–70% |

## 6. Technology Readiness Level (TRL)

**SkillCraft targets TRL 4–5:**

- **TRL 4**: Technology validated in lab environment → SkillCraft: schema + triggers + mock data validated locally
- **TRL 5**: Technology validated in relevant environment → SkillCraft: prototype API tested with synthetic user workflows, concurrency-safe bid acceptance verified

**Not TRL 6+ (pilot deployment)** because:
- No live payment gateway integration (UPI/NEFT)
- No mobile-first UI (Vite React, desktop-focused)
- No SMS/voice support for low-digital-literacy users

## 7. Impact Projection

### Artisan Impact

- **Current Earnings**: ₹100–300/hour; lose 30–50% to middleman = ₹50–150/hour net
- **With SkillCraft**: ₹100–300/hour; lose 5–7% commission = ₹93–285/hour net
- **Annual Uplift** (assuming 40 hrs/week, 48 weeks/year): **₹83,520–282,240/year** per artisan

### Scale Impact (10 Million Rural Artisans)

- **1% Adoption** (100,000 artisans) earning average ₹180/hour:
  - Gross earnings retained: **₹13.82 billion** vs. **₹6.91 billion** without platform
  - Uplift: **₹6.91 billion/year** (50% more retained by artisans)

## Conclusion

SkillCraft Micro-Jobs fills a critical gap in India's informal economy by providing **the first digital platform built specifically for rural micro-artisans**. By eliminating middlemen, enabling transparent bidding, and establishing verifiable trust through transaction history, SkillCraft empowers artisans to earn meaningfully while giving employers access to vetted, rated skilled labor at fair prices.

This solves **Tracks T9 (Education & Human Skills)** and **T12 (Livelihood & Social Entrepreneurship)** by formalizing, rating, and connecting rural skills to urban opportunity—a direct intervention in bridging urban-rural digital and economic divide.
